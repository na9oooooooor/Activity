## Activity targets

The default adult targets are 150 moderate-equivalent aerobic
minutes and 2 strength days per rolling seven activity days.

Users may configure personal targets and reset them to the defaults.

When custom targets are active, the app says “your targets.” It does
not describe a custom target as the general health guideline.

The general adult reference remains visible in the calculation
explanation.


## Apple Exercise Time reconciliation

Apple Exercise Time may include formal workouts and activity outside
workouts.

For each activity day:

classified clock minutes =
moderate workout minutes + vigorous workout minutes

supplemental Apple Exercise =
max(0, Apple Exercise Time - classified clock minutes)

moderate-equivalent total =
moderate workout minutes
+ supplemental Apple Exercise
+ 2 × vigorous workout minutes

Supplemental Apple Exercise is treated as moderate. It is never
doubled because it does not provide a reliable moderate-versus-
vigorous split.


## Activity-day boundary

Users can start their activity day between midnight and 8:00 AM.

A workout belongs to the activity day on which it starts. Activity
before the configured boundary belongs to the preceding activity day.

Changing the boundary deletes rebuildable daily summaries and daily
check-ins. Imported workouts and workout classifications remain
preserved.

The configured calendar and time zone handle daylight-saving
transitions automatically. The app does not provide a manual
daylight-saving setting.
